## be down for

- 中文意图：愿意参加或尝试某事；有兴趣
- 常用搭配：be down for a walk / be down for trying something new
- 自然例句：
  - I'm down for a walk after dinner.
  - Are you down for trying that new restaurant?
- 使用提示：原型通常说 be down for；比较随意，适合熟人间交流。
- 来源：你主动补充的表达（2026-09-29）。
- 状态：素材候选；尚未通过实际口语使用验证。

### 5Ad 场景卡 · 英文场景与挖空句

本次学习核心：`down for`。原文中的组合保留在例句中。

#### 场景 1 · down for

A friend suggests going for a hike this weekend. You like the idea and enthusiastically agree to join.

**回想句**

I'm ______ a hike this weekend.

**表达**：down for

**这个场景下的意思**：愿意参加；乐意做某事

**例句与译文**

1. I'm down for a hike this weekend.
   这周末去徒步，我很乐意。
2. I'm down for a spontaneous trip, as long as we can get back by Sunday.
   我愿意来一场说走就走的旅行，只要周日之前能回来就行。
3. Are you down for helping out at the charity event this weekend?
   你愿意这周末去慈善活动帮忙吗？

You describe the impression someone gives through the way they speak or behave. Their actual personality or intentions may be different from that impression.

   演讲时，她给人的印象是自信而热情。

使用提示：come across as + 形容词 / 名词；也可说 an emotion / message comes across，指感情或意思传达出来。
